Maine 7 Din Me Kaise Apni Website Google Pe Layi

By Salman | The Untold Life

Doston, 7 din pehle mere paas kuch nahi tha. Bas ek sapna tha ki meri bhi ek website ho. Aur aaj jab Google pe theuntoldlife.vercel.app search karta hu, toh meri website pehle number pe aati hai. Ye koi jadoo nahi, meri mehnat hai.

Day 1-2: Website Banana Sikha

Maine Spck Editor pe pehli baar HTML likha. Darr lag raha tha. Lekin raat ke 10 baje daily coding karke maine apna homepage banaya.

Day 3-4: Vercel Pe Deploy Kiya

Website banana ek baat hai, usko live karna dusri baat. Vercel ne meri help ki. Bas GitHub se connect kiya aur meri site live ho gayi.

Day 5-6: Sabse Mushkil Kaam - Sitemap

Mujhe laga sab ho gaya, lekin fir pata chala Google ko sitemap chahiye hota hai. Maine sitemap.html aur sitemap.xml banaye. Ek baar toh file ka naam galat rakh diya - sidemap likh diya! Phir sahi kiya aur sab theek ho gaya.

Day 7: Google Pe Index Ho Gayi!

Aaj subah jab maine Google Search Console me check kiya, toh meri site indexed thi. Aur Google pe search karne pe pehle number pe aa rahi thi. Us khushi ko main shabdon me nahi bata sakta.

Aap Bhi Kar Sakte Ho

Agar main Purnia, Bihar ka ek ladka karke dikha sakta hu, toh aap bhi kar sakte ho. Bas 3 cheeze chahiye: Roz ka 1 ghanta, Spck Editor, aur haar na manne ka jazba.
