🪔 Diwali Shayari

Ek diya mere naam ka bhi jalana, jab tum Diwali manana...

Diwali sirf roshni ka tyohaar nahi, dilo ko jodne ka ehsaas hai. Ghar se door rehne walo ke liye har diya ek yaad ban jata hai.

"Ghar ki kami mehsoos hoti hai jab diya jalta hai,
Maa ki yaad aati hai jab koi muskurata hai...
Diwali toh sab manate hai khushi se,
Par pardesi ka dil har baar thoda sa jalta hai."
"Ek diya mere naam ka bhi jalana,
Jab tum apni Diwali manana,
Bhool jana gile shikwe saare,
Bas ek baar mujhe bhi yaad karna..."
"Diwali ki roshni me sab kuch chamakta hai,
Bas ek kami hai jo dil ko khatakta hai,
Ghar se door hu is baar bhi,
Par dil har pal ghar ko tarasta hai."

Ye blog un sab ke liye hai jo Diwali pe ghar se door hai. Aap akela nahi ho.
